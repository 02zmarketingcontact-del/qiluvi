# QiInsight — Vietnam Investment Decision Demonstration
**Type: DEMONSTRATION. Not a delivered client engagement.**
**Evidence status: METHOD ONLY; external sector data not yet validated.**

## Fictional board question
Should an overseas mid-market manufacturer fund a 90-day Vietnam market-entry validation project?

## Executive recommendation (conditional)
Authorize a limited discovery stage, **not** full market entry, only if a named budget owner, product definition and risk tolerance exist. This is a process recommendation, not an investment recommendation.

## Decision framework
| Gate | Required evidence | Failure response |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Comparable category demand, source/date and local buyer checks | Defer market-size claims |
| Economics | Landed cost, channel margin, working capital assumptions | Reprice or stop |
| Distribution | Partner identity, reach, capability and verification | Pilot without exclusivity |
| Regulation | Current sector-specific rules reviewed by qualified adviser | Hold launch |
| Execution | 90-day owners, budget, milestones | Do not approve scale-up |

## 90-day illustrative plan
Days 1–30: scope, source register, segment hypotheses, competitive landscape.
Days 31–60: consenting buyer/partner interviews and preliminary cost model.
Days 61–90: triangulation, pilot design, decision memo and go/no-go gate.

## Outputs
Decision memo; evidence matrix; competitor map; scenario workbook specification; interview guide; risk register.

## Risks and limitations
No actual customer interviews, sector TAM, vendor quotes, legal opinion or realized outcome are claimed. Source-specific citations must be added before this becomes an independent research report.
