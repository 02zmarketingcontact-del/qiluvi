# QiBridge — China-to-Vietnam Market Entry Decision Report
**DEMONSTRATION — hypothetical Chinese manufacturer, not a real client or verified partner network.**

## Decision
Compare cross-border export, non-exclusive distributor, commercial agent, JV and local subsidiary as *options*, not interchangeable legal structures.

| Option | Operational attraction | Key validation before approval |
|---|---|---|
| Export to importer | Low initial local fixed footprint | Product importability, duties, importer of record |
| Distributor | Local sales coverage | Credit, channel access, exclusivity terms, performance |
| Agent | Potentially lighter asset commitment | Agency contract, tax, representation and applicable rules |
| JV | Local co-investment | Governance, ownership, related-party and exit risks |
| Subsidiary | Greater direct control | Sector conditions, incorporation, labor, tax and setup costs |

## Partner screening scorecard (unscored)
Registration and beneficial ownership; business scope; product fit; coverage; customer references; financial resilience; warehousing/service capacity; conflicts; compliance and consent to due diligence. Unknown fields remain UNKNOWN, never a passing score.

## 90-day plan (illustrative)
- Days 1–30: product HS classification hypothesis, demand segments, import/regulatory questions and route shortlist.
- Days 31–60: qualified candidate sourcing, consent-based screening and localized unit economics.
- Days 61–90: pilot scope, draft commercial terms, legal/tax specialist review, board decision.

## Cost model inputs
Staff time, research and translation, travel, qualified advisers, candidate checks, import/logistics assumptions, CRM and contingency. **COST_INPUT_REQUIRED**: no supplier quotations or market rates established.

## Go / no-go conditions
No exclusive distribution without verification and performance milestones; no local incorporation recommendation without current sector-specific professional review; no assertion of partner introductions until actually documented.

## Evidence and limitations
This is a methodology demonstration. It does not claim interviews, due diligence, negotiated terms, regulatory approvals or successful launches.
