# QILUVI MASTER PLAN — V6.0
**Baseline date:** 18 October 2026 · **Owner:** Founder · **Coverage:** QiInsight, QiBridge, QiReal, QiBuild, QiGrow; QiMap remains an independent research product.

## End goal
Build a credible research-driven Vietnam market intelligence and business expansion practice: client decisions → sourced research → defined professional tasks → scoped commercial packages → truthful credentials → multilingual website → measurable service operations.

## Non-negotiable publication guardrails
- QILUVI slogan: CLARITY BEYOND BORDERS. Traditional Chinese: 看見更真實的越南. Founder Felix 陳歸, +84 966 108 018.
- Do not invent office, licensed entity, years of experience, certified capacity, case studies, customer reviews, price realizations or revenue.
- Research public sources with publisher, URL, publication/review date, scope, method, rights and confidence.
- Use confidence categories VERIFIED, SUPPORTED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN. Customer pain entries still need buyer validation.
- Reserve legal/tax, statutory engineering/construction supervision and regulated valuation to appropriately qualified professionals.
- QiMap is a separate product and repository; reference asking prices never become certified transactions without direct evidence.
- Data from third-party sites are research references; do not bulk republish listings, photos, private group content or copyrighted datasets.
- Significant website changes go through branch → PR → independent QA → merge → Pages verification.

## 30 / 60 / 90 day stage gates
**Day 0–30:** Publish and confirm language/navigation paths; run real buyer discovery (at least five decision-maker interviews per service line if consent and access allow); obtain legal/technical specialist directory with verified qualifications; quote Essential pilot from actual labor/partner rates.

**Day 31–60:** Sell or deliberately test limited-scope paid discovery products; track paid/unpaid status, hours, rework and margin. Improve scope templates; collect source-rights clearances; validate offer/channel demand by buyer feedback and actual proposals.

**Day 61–90:** Review pilot profitability and outcomes with permission; publish only verified case studies; establish refresh cadence and owner responsibility for research and optional subscriptions; decide which 2–3 services merit expansion.

## Decision priorities
Before competing on breadth: provide 1) traceable research, 2) decision-ready outputs, 3) competent professional handoffs, 4) transparent commercial scope. Avoid features that do not move a client decision.

## Operating safeguards
No 12 independent AI agents or continuous background operations are claimed by this file; roles are functional responsibilities. No scheduling system exists solely because a plan or workflow YAML was written. Current status is recorded in EXECUTIVE_STATUS.md.
