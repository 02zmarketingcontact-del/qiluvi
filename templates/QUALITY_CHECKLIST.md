# Quality Review Checklist

- [ ] Material numbers have source, date, definition and confidence.
- [ ] No promise of investment return, ROAS, permits or transaction certainty.
- [ ] Legal, property and construction credential roles verified.
- [ ] No customer personal data or secret in public repo.
- [ ] Publication rights for all brands, images and cases.
- [ ] Four language and mobile contact/navigation link checks.
- [ ] Each output has owner, revision, limits and acceptance.
