# Pricing & Unit Economics — control framework

No public fee or market-price assertion is made. Cost and sell price require real capacity, quotes and tax review.

**Direct costs =** Σ(actual role hours × approved fully loaded hourly cost) + purchased research/data + external partner quotes + travel + field expense + per-project software.

**All-in delivery costs =** Direct costs + attributable project management and QA hours + allocated overhead + approved risk contingency.

**Pre-tax quote =** All-in delivery costs / (1 − selected project margin target). Tax and pass-through charges are handled per contract and applicable requirements.

| Option | Character | Safeguard | Price disclosure |
|---|---|---|---|
| LEAN | Narrow fixed-scope desktop deliverable, founder + analyst | Geography and sample ceiling | Fixed only after costing 3 repeatable deliveries |
| STANDARD | Multi-stakeholder research and verified options | QA, partner and travel budget | Scope-based quote |
| PREMIUM | Milestone PMO, field coordination, expert partners | Change register and monthly capacity cap | Milestone or retainer |

**Illustrative arithmetic only (not a market quote):** 20 cost units of labor/partners plus 5 overhead/contingency units, at a hypothetical 25% contribution margin = 25 / 0.75 = 33.33 internal units. Replace with actual VND costs and signed third-party prices.

**Approval gate:** Scope → hours → partner capability → verified cost → commercial margin → legal/ethics review → quotation version → client acceptance. No success fee on regulated activity without licensed counsel review. No promise of returns, permits, ROAS or revenue.
