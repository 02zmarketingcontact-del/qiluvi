About

Entering a new market is difficult not only because of geographic distance. Market figures may use inconsistent definitions, introductions may lack proper verification and operating sites may not meet the real business need.

QILUVI is designed to narrow the distance between understanding Vietnam, selecting a strategy and coordinating work on the ground. We start from a specific business question, document evidence and assumptions, and translate decisions into owned tasks and clear deliverables.

QiInsight, QiBridge, QiReal, QiBuild and QiGrow address distinct parts of a company's development journey. QiMap remains an independent apartment-data research product; no map point alone establishes a transaction price, legal conclusion or foreign ownership entitlement.

We do not claim every specialty as in-house or promise financial outcomes. Credibility comes from source transparency, precise scope, qualified partners and explicit handling of what remains unknown.
