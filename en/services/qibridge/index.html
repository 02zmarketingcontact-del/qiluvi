Frequently asked questions

Can QILUVI guarantee an incorporation permit?

No. Incorporation, legal and tax opinions, and approvals require qualified providers and official processes.

Does a partner list mean due diligence is complete?

No. Each prospect retains an evidence level and any further field or legal checks.

What is needed for discovery?

Product specifications, intended customers, geographic focus, 12-month goals and resource assumptions.
