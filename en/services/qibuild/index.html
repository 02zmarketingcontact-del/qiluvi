Decisions that need clarity

  • Foreign design documents may not be reviewed for applicable local standards.
  • BOQs and bids omit different scope items, making comparison misleading.
  • Variations and material substitutions proceed without documented approval.
  • Schedule, inspections and handover responsibilities are unclear.

Who we help

  • Foreign project owners coordinating design and construction in Vietnam
  • Companies fitting out factories, offices and showrooms
  • Asset owners requiring budget and contractor transparency

What we actually do

  7. Coordinate records and qualified inspection/handover checklists.

Frequently asked questions

Are you a licensed general contractor?

We do not claim licensed contractor or statutory supervisor status without verified evidence; those roles are assigned to qualified parties.

Can you work with China-origin drawings?

We can coordinate bilingual design interfaces and flag drawings for qualified local-code assessment.

How do you manage cost variations?

Agreed baseline, comparative BOQ, written change control and approval records.
