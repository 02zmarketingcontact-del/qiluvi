03

What we actually do

  1. Specify asset class, buyer eligibility and search geography.
  2. Compile candidates and reconcile project names and development phases.
  3. Separate asking references, modeled values and verified transactions.
  4. Review accessibility, utilities, operations and revenue drivers.
  5. Build sensitivity scenarios for cashflow or total occupancy cost.
  6. List documents requiring independent legal/valuation review.
  7. Rank the shortlist and plan inspections and decision gates.
