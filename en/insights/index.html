Vietnam Market Entry / October 2026

Reviewed: 18 Oct 2026

2025 · Foreign Investment

For 2025 Vietnam's statistical agency reported US$38.42bn of registered inward FDI and US$27.62bn of disbursed FDI. The figures describe different measures.

H1 2026 · Foreign Investment

For H1 2026 the statistical agency reported US$34.65bn of registered inward FDI and US$13.03bn disbursed. A half-year figure is not a full-year forecast.

Vietnam · Distribution

The U.S. International Trade Administration's guide updated 23 April 2026 emphasizes distributor/agent diligence, permits, resources and written contractual obligations.

Savills' Vietnam Q3 2026 real estate brief, published 15 October, describes HCMC apartment demand as affected by financing costs and affordability.
