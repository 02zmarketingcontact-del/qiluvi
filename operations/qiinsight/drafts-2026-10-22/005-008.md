### vi-VN — Việt Nam nêu sáu hướng cải cách thị trường chứng khoán

### en — Vietnam outlines six stock-market reform priorities

At an October 21 securities seminar, a regulator described six reform priorities following FTSE Russell's market-classification change. Reported market capitalisation stood near VND10.3 quadrillion on September 30. Investors should distinguish policy intentions from implemented rules.
