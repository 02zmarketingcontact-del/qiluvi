en | Forest-boundary mapping may support EUDR supply-chain checks
Newly publicised forest-boundary information may help Vietnamese exporters trace sourcing areas. For EU-bound timber, coffee and rubber, mapping is an input to compliance, not automatic proof that a shipment meets all EUDR requirements.
