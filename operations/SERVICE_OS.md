# Service Operating System / Lean SOP

These roles are organizational responsibilities and do not assert concurrent independent AI agents or continuously running operations.

| Stage | Operational work | Exit | Control/KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lead | Receive channel enquiry, record consent and topic | Lead ID and next action | Reply timeliness |
| 2. Qualification | Confirm sector, buyer, budget owner and urgency | Qualification brief | Qualified inquiry rate |
| 3. Discovery | Ask recent real pain event, prior alternatives and decision deadline | Jobs-to-be-done brief | Discovery-to-scope rate |
| 4. Scope | Select WBS tasks, partner split and exclusions | Signed scope draft | Unambiguous regulated roles |
| 5. Proposal | Cost staff hours/QA/data/travel/vendors, calculate margin and taxes | Proposal revision | Delivery economics reviewed |
| 6. Contract | Document data rights, payment, limits and confidentiality | Executed contract | No unauthorized work |
| 7. Kickoff | Assign task owners and decision gate schedule | Kickoff minutes | 100% workstream ownership |
| 8. Delivery | Execute research/work, update log and written change orders | Versioned deliverables | Milestone and QA progress |
| 9. QA | Check claims, data sources, legal/partner obligations, accessibility | QA record | No critical defects |
| 10. Acceptance | Review every deliverable against acceptance statement | Signed acceptance or exceptions | Acceptance elapsed time |
| 11. Follow-up | Record lessons and unresolved client decisions | Follow-up note | Client feedback when available |
| 12. Renewal | Discuss next bounded engagement only if needed | Renewal SOW | Measured repeat economics |

## RACI
- Founder / Business Development: accountable for client qualification, final scope, ethics and pricing.
- Research Analyst: responsible for source registers, evidence levels and factual conclusions.
- Coordinator: responsible for RFI, project log, partner schedule and change management.
- Brand/Web: responsible for multi-language public messaging, website and CRM configuration.
- Professional partners: solely responsible for regulated opinions, certified design, statutory supervision or valuation under valid agreements.
- Quality Reviewer: independently reviews claims, source rights, contractual limits and deliverables.
- Client: owns business decisions, approvals, authorized input and final acceptance.

## Critical stop gates
Do not proceed without: legitimate source/use rights, confidentiality handling, disclosure of partner conflict, credential verification for regulated duties, and agreement on deliverable acceptance. Do not equate completion of tasks with a guaranteed commercial result.
