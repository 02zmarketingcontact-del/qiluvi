# Independent Research Note — Vietnam Market Entry 2026

**Type:** Independent Research; **owner:** QILUVI research; **updated:** 2026-10-18; **not commissioned client work**.

## Verified public signals
- Vietnam NSO's January 2026 release states 2025 inbound registered investment of **US$38.42bn** and realized FDI of **US$27.62bn**, two distinct metrics and not interchangeable. [M02](https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/data-and-statistics/2026/01/press-release-social-economic-situation-in-the-fourth-quarter-and-2025/)
- NSO H1 2026 release reports **US$34.65bn** registered inward FDI and **US$13.03bn** disbursed in the first six months of 2026. H1 registered investment cannot be directly extrapolated to full-year new projects. [M03](https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/data-and-statistics/2026/08/press-release-socio-economic-performance-in-the-q2-and-in-the-first-half-of-2026/)
- U.S. ITA's April 2026 Vietnam guide distinguishes agents/distributors and recommends counterparty due diligence, permits, facilities, capital and clearly documented rights. [M01](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/vietnam-distribution-and-sales-channels)
- Savills' 15 Oct 2026 Q3 brief describes HCMC apartment buyer demand as affected by borrowing costs/affordability, and differentiated performance by sector. [M04](https://www.savills.com/research_articles/255800/239332-1)

## Analysis (QILUVI interpretation, not source statements)
Foreign-market opportunities do not imply that every segment can earn adequate returns. The actionable decision is which city, buyer, channel, investment vehicle and partner pass a documented feasibility gate. Entry readiness should consider (i) market evidence, (ii) channel diligence, (iii) local delivery cost, (iv) licensing checked by specialists, and (v) realistic milestones.

## Proposed decision template
| Gate | Required evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Market | Segment demand and rival proxy with source date | Proceed to field testing or revise |
| Entry | Agent/distributor/entity model reviewed with legal counsel | Choose feasible entry route |
| Execution | Named owners, resource quotes and go-live dependencies | Fund stage one or defer |

## Limitations
This note is a desk review, not an original survey, demand forecast, investment recommendation or legal opinion. No client mandate or transaction is claimed. Need sector-specific primary sources and buyer interviews before vertical conclusions.
