## Service Gap Analysis
- **QiInsight:** niche bilingual *decision memo*, source ledger and updateable scope for smaller foreign teams; not a replacement for panel research.
- **QiBridge:** before incorporation, define buyer/channel/partner evidence and a 90-day action-owner matrix; legal, tax and licenses outsourced to appropriately qualified providers.
- **QiReal:** project identity, asking-vs-transaction labels, total occupancy cost, rental and legal verification gates; QiMap only as research product.
- **QiBuild:** transparency in bid comparison, variation logs, procurement dependencies and qualified-party signoff.
- **QiGrow:** practical translation-to-localization workflow, B2B/B2C split, CRM handoff and consent-aware measurement.

**Differentiation hypothesis:** one bilingual evidence file and accountable delivery handoff across five services. This is positioning to test, *not* a proven customer preference or established competitive superiority.
