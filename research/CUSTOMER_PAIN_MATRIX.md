## Interview proof gates
1. 5+ decision-maker interviews per service line across distinct segments; ask for most recent episode and monetary/time consequences.
2. Log evidence of budget owner, timing, prior spend, existing vendors, redacted artifacts and decision route; obtain consent.
3. Validate two paid discovery pilots per promising line; measure gross margin and revision load.
4. Recompute scoring from observed data; do not convert qualitative guesses into demand statistics.
