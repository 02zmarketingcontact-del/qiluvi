# QiInsight — Decision-led Research Dossier V6.1
Status: DRAFT / HYPOTHESIS. 18 October 2026.

## Buyer and decision
Target: international manufacturer or investor evaluating a Vietnam market. Decision owner: CEO / investment committee. Buying trigger: initial entry budget or board review. This buyer framing is an analytical hypothesis, not an interview result.

## Root problem and service mechanism
Contradictory market sizes arise when product category, geography, units and time periods differ. A source register and reconciled market definition precede TAM/SAM/SOM. National investment inflows cannot prove a specific industry's addressable demand.

## Executable research sequence
1. Freeze decision question, product scope, geography and as-of date.
2. Collect official statistics, customs/trade data where licensed, industry reports and public company filings.
3. Log publisher, date, original definition, URL, license, limits and confidence.
4. Reconcile incompatible figures without averaging incompatible denominators.
5. Map competitors and substitute products, noting missing coverage.
6. Build base/upside/downside scenarios and explicit assumptions.
7. Deliver executive decision memo with go / test / no-go gates.

## Demonstration deliverable specification
Executive summary; scope; source comparison; demand drivers; competition; regulation watchlist; scenario assumptions; decision gate; evidence register. No unverified numerical market size or implied client case.

## Acceptance criteria
Every material numeric claim is traceable to a dated source; assumptions distinguish facts from interpretation; reviewer can reproduce calculations. Interview and price validation remain NOT_STARTED.
